01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE SCREEN

A little about me.

FIG. 01 A glimpse of me ✨

Hello! I'm Zoe Katelyn M. Villariba, but you can call me Zoe or Katkat.

I'm a second-year BA Multimedia Studies student and currently one of the Directors of Publicity of UP Cinema. I'm 19 years old and from Quezon City. I enjoy exploring my creativity through film, photography, editing and song writing.

I consider myself an open-minded person who values self-expression, personal growth and meaningful connections with others. I'm always looking for opportunities to challenge myself, gain new perspectives and become a better version of myself. As I continue my journey as a multimedia student, I hope to discover more about who I am and what I can contribute through my work and experiences.
